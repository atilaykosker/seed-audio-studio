GRADE 3
A Rainy Day at Home
Characters:
Rio the Parrot
Bunny the Rabbit
Nico the Squirrel

Page 1
Narrator: It is raining outside. The animals can’t go to the park.
Bunny: “Oh no! It’s raining again.”
Rio: “Don’t worry. We can play at home!”
Illustration (Turkish): Yağmur damlaları pencereye vuruyor, Bunny üzgün, Rio kanat çırpıyor.

Page 2
Narrator: Bunny is in the kitchen.
Bunny: “I have got carrots. Do you want some?”
Nico: “Yes, I’m hungry. Let’s eat together.”
Illustration (Turkish): Bunny havuç çıkarıyor, Nico masada oturmuş bekliyor.

Page 3
Narrator: Rio brings a board game.
Rio: “Look! I have got a game. Let’s play.”
Nico: “Great! I like games.”
Illustration (Turkish): Rio bir kutu oyun çıkarıyor, Bunny ve Nico masaya yaklaşıyor.

Page 4
Narrator: They are playing happily.
Bunny: “Haha! I am winning now.”
Rio: “No, I am winning!”
Nico: “We are all having fun.”
Illustration (Turkish): Üç hayvan masa oyunu oynuyor, gülüyorlar.

Page 5 
Narrator: Suddenly, Bunny drops a carrot on the game.
Nico: “Oh no! The carrot is on the board!”
Rio: “Hahaha! Now the carrot is the winner!”
Illustration (Turkish): Havuç oyun tahtasının üstünde, Rio ve Nico gülüyor.

Page 6
Narrator: The rain stops.
Bunny: “Look! The sun is out.”
Rio: “Let’s go outside now.”
Nico: “Yes, but bring the carrot winner too!”
Illustration (Turkish): Yağmur durmuş, güneş çıkmış, hayvanlar dışarı koşuyor, Bunny havucu taşıyor.
